### 1.4 Margin of Safety Principle

#### Multi-Timeframe Analysis Requirement

The margin of safety principle requires undervaluation across short, mid, and long-term timeframes before bulk investment.

**Entry Criterion**: Only bulk invest when margin of safety exists at ALL three levels simultaneously.

#### Margin of Safety Calculation Framework

**Formula**:
```
Total Margin of Safety = External Margin + Internal Margin

Where:
- External Margin = (Market Peak - Current Price) / Market Peak × 100%
- Internal Margin = Unrealized Gains / Deployed Capital × 100%
```

**Target Range**: Maintain 5-7% total margin of safety across portfolio

**Deployment Triggers**:
| Margin of Safety | Action |
|------------------|--------|
| > 10% | Deploy additional capital |
| 5-7% | Maintain current allocation |
| < 5% | Reduce new deployments |

#### Practical Example

$200K invested with 2.69% unrealized gains + 3% market discount = 5.69% total margin of safety

**Current Market Assessment**: Zero margin of safety in current conditions (NASDAQ up 98.2% from Jan 2020-Dec 2022) suggests shift from bulk investment to tactical trading approaches.

- Convert notional gains to realized profits through strategic exits
- Withdraw **100% of gains** to transform into "real profits"

- Timeline between stages varies from 3 months to 5 years

Internal Margin = Unrealized Gains / Deployed Capital × 100%

#### Deployment Triggers

|------------------|--------|
| > 10% | Deploy additional capital |
| 5-7% | Maintain current allocation |
