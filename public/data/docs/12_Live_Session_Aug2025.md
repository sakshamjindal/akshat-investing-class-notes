Real estate syndication strategy offers significant cost savings by eliminating builder margins and marketing expenses. The instructor's analysis reveals that standard builder models include 33% for land acquisition, 33% for construction, 33% for marketing, plus 20% profit margin, totaling 120 units of cost to consumers.

![Figure: Builder cost structure analysis for syndication strategy](frame:scene_0331.jpg)

![Figure: Syndication cost structure and savings calculation](frame:periodic_0616.jpg)
